Platform & Goals

My Priorities

Concrete goals to strengthen education, expand support for our students, and bring transparency and accountability to the Board.

What I Will Fight For

Goals When Elected

1

Strengthen & Update Existing Programs

Update and strengthen existing programs and implement additional initiatives that further support our children's education and well-being.

2

Expand Special Education Support

Introduce a little-to-no-cost program that will make a real difference for students who need it most:

  • Expand and strengthen the Special Education programs
  • Hire additional Special Education staff who are certified as Special Education teachers
  • Enable teachers to refer students for special education testing without administrator approval
3

Stop Non-Credentialed Teacher Hiring

Work to stop the hiring of non-credentialed classroom teachers under the guise of emergency credentials. Our students deserve qualified, certified educators in every classroom.

4

Expand Peer Tutoring to All Lower Grades

Expand the Peer Tutoring program — which I originally helped establish — to include all lower grades, giving younger students access to the same academic support at no cost to parents.

5

Monthly Board News Updates

Have the Board publish a monthly news update in the Silver Lakes Ledger to keep parents informed about Board of Trustees activities and decisions that affect their children.

6

Update Classroom Materials

Review and update classroom textbooks and software programs so students work with current, relevant materials that prepare them for the future.

7

Enhance the Grant Writing Program

Enhance and expand the Grant Writing program I introduced when I was the Board president in 2018 — securing free funding for programs that directly benefit our students and district.

Every decision I make will center on one question:
"Is this the best thing for our students?"

I will remain fully visible, accessible, and responsive to parents, teachers, and community members. Together, we can build a stronger foundation for Helendale's children.
