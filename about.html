What I Accomplished as Board President

During my tenure as Board President in 2018, I was instrumental in developing programs that directly benefited students and saved the district significant resources.

After-School Peer Tutoring Program

Established the after-school Peer Tutoring program at no cost to parents, giving students access to additional academic support from their peers.

Grant Writing Program

Developed the district's Grant program that secured substantial free funding for programs that directly benefited our students and the district.

Portable Classrooms Acquisition

Secured the acquisition of portable classrooms at school sites, saving the district over $100,000 in yearly rental expenses.

High School College Credit Program

Launched the High School College Program, which allows high school students to earn college-transferable credit as well as high school credit. Students who participate can earn 40 or more transferable college credits at zero cost to parents or students — a program that can result in thousands of dollars in free college credits.
